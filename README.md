### 🍕 Pizza Sales Dashboard

Developed an interactive **Pizza Sales Dashboard in Microsoft Power BI** to analyze sales performance, customer demand, and product-level performance. The dashboard provides a clear view of key business metrics and helps identify the products and periods contributing most to overall sales.

The dashboard focuses on **Total Revenue, Total Quantity Sold, Best-Selling Pizzas, Worst-Selling Pizzas, Highest-Demand Products, and Sales Performance**. It compares pizza products based on their **revenue and quantity sold**, making it easier to identify the strongest and weakest performers.

### Key Insights
- **Best Sellers:** Identifies pizzas generating the highest sales and revenue.
- **Worst Sellers:** Highlights underperforming pizzas with comparatively lower sales and quantity.
- **Highest Demand:** Shows which pizza products are ordered most frequently.
- **Sales Revenue:** Analyzes revenue generated across different pizza products.
- **Quantity Sold:** Tracks the number of pizzas sold and compares product performance.
- **Product Performance:** Helps understand which pizzas contribute most to overall business performance.

This dashboard transforms raw pizza sales data into **interactive and actionable business insights**, supporting better decisions related to **menu optimization, inventory planning, marketing strategies, and revenue growth**
